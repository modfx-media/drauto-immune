# Dr. Autoimmune SEO audit

**Site:** https://drautoimmune.com  
**Date:** 30 September 2026  
**Scope:** Live sitemap (131 URLs), DataForSEO domain and keyword data, Google Search Console, and a live sample of location pages, learn articles, condition pillars, and blog posts.

The whole-site technical crawl timed out, so on-page depth is a representative sample rather than a 60-check pass of all 131 URLs. Keyword and index findings come from DataForSEO and Search Console directly.

## Summary

The keywords this rebuild was written for are in DataForSEO. The site is not ranking for them.

DataForSEO shows `drautoimmune.com` ranking for 57 US keywords, at an average position of 41.7, worth about 127 estimated organic visits a month. Only 3 of those keywords are on page 1. Search Console from 1 July through 29 September 2026 is almost entirely brand traffic. `/learn/` and `/areas-we-serve/` have zero impressions in that window, and a query check back to 1 April 2026 also returned zero impressions for the intended phrases.

The location template is thin. State hubs are about 480 body words and read as the same page with the state name swapped. The 10 learn articles are unique and still too short to win the queries they target. Google has discovered a few of them and has not indexed them.

- No JSON-LD on the parsed page.

- H1 is “Telehealth Autoimmune & Functional Medicine Care in Texas.” It does not use “doctor.”

ANA learn-article findings:

- 726 body words. Medically structured and unique against its sibling learn pages.
- No structured data detected, no author detected in the parse, inbound links counted at zero.
- Google’s coverage state is “Discovered – currently not indexed.”

Cluster read: the 61 location URLs were published together, sit in a narrow word-count band, and change mainly by place name. That is a medium-to-high scaled-content risk for a medical site, even though 61 pages is a small set. The learn articles are a separate, stronger set. They still need more depth before they can compete.

Homepage quality check: indexed, self-referencing canonical, `MedicalBusiness` schema present, Open Graph complete, single H1 (“We Are Autoimmune Wellness Specialists”). No `BreadcrumbList` schema.

## What to do first

1. **Deepen the 10 learn articles, starting with ANA and IBS.** Those are the intended keywords with confirmed volume (1,600 and 590) whose URLs Google has already discovered.
2. **Add a physician byline, citations, and medical schema** (`MedicalWebPage` or `FAQPage`) on those articles. They are being skipped with no author and no structured data.
3. **Keep Denver. Noindex or fold the other 49 state hubs into the areas hub.** There is no Ads volume for those state phrases, the copy is the same page, and Texas is still unknown to Google.
4. **Leave the 400 condition-by-state URLs noindexed.** They are the thinnest set and are already out of the sitemap.
5. **Put the next editorial effort into posts that already rank.** Leaky gut (22,200 volume, positions 25–36) and the positive-ANA post are where non-brand traffic already exists.
